## Release boundary

This release is a complete foundation, not a claim of perfect editorial judgement. It is ready for structured use and regression testing. Production confidence must be earned through real assignments, accepted and rejected edits, false-positive review and cross-platform evaluation.

## Known limitations

- Regression fixtures are specified but not yet scored automatically by a model-agnostic evaluator.
- Factual verification quality depends on source access and the active platform's browsing or connector capabilities.
- Voice profiles require representative, consented source samples.
- Presentation visual production remains delegated to presentation-building tools.
- Book continuity requires project-level manuscript maps and registries.

## Exit criteria for 1.0

- At least 50 benchmark passages across forms and authorship models
- Documented human preference testing
- Cross-platform adapter tests
- False-positive and semantic-drift thresholds
- Stable disclosure and provenance policy
- Packaging and installation tests on supported Skill environments
