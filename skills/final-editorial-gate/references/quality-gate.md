# Quality Gate for the Gate
A decision is valid only when artefacts and versions are listed, checks apply to the final draft, blockers cite evidence/rules, decision states are used consistently, improvements are separate from release conditions, approver and next action are named, no rewriting occurred and waivers are explicit.

Self-check: Am I passing polished prose with weak evidence? Blocking a human judgement rather than escalating? Did post-verification edits stale upstream work? Can another reviewer reproduce the decision?
