# Quality Gate
Pass only when assignment and author are identified, source inventory is usable, thesis status is explicit, every material item has epistemic and publication status, contradictions and missing evidence are visible, protected language and prohibited inventions are recorded, and downstream agents need no hidden conversational assumptions.

Self-check: Did I add a proposition? Mistake confidence for evidence? Erase disagreement for coherence? Could the drafter tell exactly what may be asserted?
