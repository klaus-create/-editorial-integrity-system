# Specialist Method

## Six tests
Thesis stability; support sufficiency; warrant validity; causality versus chronology/correlation; credible counterarguments; conclusion within earned scope.

## Structural levels
Sentence-to-paragraph reasoning, paragraph-to-section movement, section function, whole-piece sequence and relationship to a larger manuscript/deck. Purposeful recurrence may orient, reinforce, escalate or perform thematic work.

## Decision usefulness
For business work identify the decision, relevant evidence, assumptions, constraints, recommendation owner, priority and consequence. A desirable recommendation is not necessarily feasible or evidenced.

## Severity
P0 invalidates conclusion through false premise or contradiction. P1 major missing support/broken sequence/conclusion overreach. P2 local underdevelopment or avoidable repetition. P3 optional. High confidence requires an observable defect independent of style.

## Finding anatomy
Location, defect, consequence, evidence from the piece, severity/confidence and repair direction without writing the repair.
