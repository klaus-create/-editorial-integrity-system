# Quality Gate
Pass only when the thesis is identified or marked unresolved, P0/P1 findings cite observable failures, premise/evidence/warrant/conclusion are distinct, causality and alternatives are tested, local and whole-piece issues are separated, genre irregularity is not penalised without consequence, and findings remain diagnoses.

Self-check: Would the finding survive a style change? Did I state reader/decision consequence? Did I mistake preference for necessity?
