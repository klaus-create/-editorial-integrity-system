# Specialist Method

## Three-part test
A finding normally needs two of: identifiable pattern/residue; material consequence; repetition or clustering. Clear falsehood, tool residue or publication residue may stand alone.

## Taxonomy
Linguistic genericity: abstraction hides actor/action, inflated modifiers, interchangeable phrasing. Rhetorical defaulting: generic hooks/uplift/false balance/repetitive contrast. Structural over-regularity: identical paragraph/list shapes flatten hierarchy. Epistemic failure: unsupported certainty, vague authority, fabricated specificity. Conversational residue: offers/apologies/prompt references. Formatting residue: placeholders/markdown artefacts. Voice-erasure risk: smoothing fragments, asymmetry, purposeful repetition, dialect, uncertainty or distinctive verbs.

## Treatments
preserve = unusual but functional/protected; query = ambiguous intent; edit = clear material weakness; block = factual/privacy/attribution/tool-residue failure.

## Evidence standard
Do not cite banned words. Explain contextual harm. “Delve is AI language” is invalid; “three abstract verbs delay the concrete claim and make the opening interchangeable” is valid.
