# Quality Gate
Pass only when sections perform defined functions, material claims trace to authorised support or judgement, uncertainty/disagreement remain visible, no experience/quote/example was invented, form-specific structure is appropriate, protected voice survives, gaps are explicit and the conclusion does not exceed the body.

Self-check: Map every material claim. Check transitions for false causality or consensus. Remove generic completion filler. Confirm the output behaves like its form.
