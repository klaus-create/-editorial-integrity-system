# Specialist Method

## Sample sufficiency defaults
Provisional: 3 substantial samples or about 1,500 words. Stable personal: 6 samples across at least 2 contexts. Institutional: 8 approved samples with known governance. Literary: enough material to observe sentence, section and chapter patterns. Downgrade uncertain authorship or heavily edited samples.

## Six layers
Rhetorical development; syntax and cadence; lexical abstraction and terminology; structural sequencing and repetition; punctuation behaviour; epistemic certainty, attribution and dissent.

## Trait test
Every trait must cite at least two independent samples, note counterevidence, compare with generic prose, define scope, state confidence and give an editing instruction. “Authoritative” is weak. “States the governing claim early, then qualifies scope before implications” is operational.

## Status
Repeated across registers = enduring. Repeated within one register = register-specific. One anomalous sample = candidate only. Equal counterevidence = context-dependent or reject. Phrase-copy dependence = imitation risk.

## Updating
Learn only from confirmed human-authored samples or explicitly accepted edits. Version profiles; never auto-learn from every generated output.
