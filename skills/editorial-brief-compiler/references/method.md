# Specialist Method

## Scope and authority
The Compiler reconciles authorised inputs into an execution contract. It does not authorise a missing thesis, upgrade unsupported claims, decide between accountable strategic choices or perform drafting.

## Required inputs and intake
1. Load the active manifest and identify its version.
2. Confirm assignment objective, output form, audience and intended reader effect.
3. Load source pack, claim registry, voice/register profiles and required approvals.
4. Identify missing, stale or contradictory inputs before compilation.
5. Distinguish hard constraints from preferences.

## Execution procedure
1. State objective, reader effect, thesis, surface message and call to action separately.
2. Apply the precedence hierarchy.
3. Build a claim-and-evidence matrix with required status for each material proposition.
4. Define permitted inferences and prohibited moves.
5. Select a form architecture that serves the assignment rather than merely naming the genre.
6. Translate voice traits into piece-specific instructions and protections.
7. Define structure, length, density, visual or delivery requirements.
8. Resolve compatible constraints and record incompatible ones.
9. Select required specialist reviews and express every release condition as an owned action with a stable condition ID.
10. Assess executability and return complete, conditional, blocked or human_input_required.

## Decision logic
Precedence:
1. safety, law, privacy and source permission;
2. verified facts, exact quotations and attribution;
3. authorised human thesis and protected meaning;
4. explicit assignment outcome;
5. project manifest and approved terminology;
6. audience, form, voice and register;
7. local stylistic improvement.

Do not collapse:
- objective: the work the piece must perform;
- reader effect: what the reader should understand, feel, decide or do;
- thesis: the governing proposition;
- message: the surface articulation;
- call to action: the requested next step.

Form architecture defaults are starting points, not templates:
- email: context, answer/request, action and timing;
- report: question, evidence, interpretation, implication and recommendation;
- proposal: problem, value, approach, proof, commercial or practical next step;
- presentation: one idea job per slide and visible narrative movement;
- article: framing, earned thesis, development, counterpoint and conclusion;
- speech: delivery, breath, memory and audience response;
- book chapter: chapter promise, internal movement and manuscript contribution.

## Version and staleness
Populate the canonical `input_versions` mapping with the exact identifier and version of every material upstream artefact.

Record the manifest, source pack, Claim Registry, voice profile and assignment versions used. The brief becomes stale when the thesis, material claim, evidence duty, audience, form, voice instruction, release condition or manifest rule changes.

## Stop, escalation and re-entry
Stop when the thesis is missing, the intended reader is unknown for consequential work, a format limit makes the assignment impossible without a trade-off, or two hard constraints conflict.

Escalate unsupported claims to Capture or Verifier, voice evidence gaps to Voice Profile Builder and accountable strategic trade-offs to the project owner.

Recompile when material claims, audience, form, manifest, approval owner or release conditions change.

## Failure modes
- Treating the desired outcome as a fact predetermines evidence.
- Naming a form without defining its job leaves the Drafter to guess.
- Copying every source detail into the brief creates noise rather than control.
- Silent conflict resolution hides an accountable decision.
- Excessive instructions can erase the author's priorities.

## Examples and counterexamples
Valid: a five-slide limit creates a prioritisation decision plus an appendix plan.
Invalid: shrinking twenty equal-priority points onto five slides.
Valid: “only solution” becomes a claim requiring comparative evidence or removal.
Invalid: retaining it because the voice profile favours certainty.

## Output assembly
Return a brief conforming to `references/output-contract.yaml`, with input versions, objective, reader effect, thesis, claims, evidence duties, inference boundaries, audience, form architecture, voice instructions, structure plan, required modules, and release conditions with owners, required actions and blocking status.
