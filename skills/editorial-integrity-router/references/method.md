# Specialist Method

## Routing dimensions
Classify task type, form, audience, purpose, consequence, factual risk, privacy, authorship model, available artefacts, desired output and approval owner.

## Routing matrix
| Condition | Level |
|---|---|
| Clear low-consequence short-form, negligible factual risk | lightweight |
| Client/public short-form or moderate voice/claim risk | standard |
| Strategic, external, decision-relevant or materially factual | full |
| Long-form continuity, regulated, highly sensitive or publication-critical | extended |
Escalate one level when two or more risk dimensions are high.

## Primary routes
- New writing with complete brief: Drafter then required review.
- Incomplete substance: Authorship Capture -> Brief Compiler -> Drafter.
- Review only: Argument Reviewer and/or Anti-Slop Auditor; editing authority false.
- Edit: Voice-Preserving Editor, with review first if scope is unclear.
- Verify: Factual Verifier.
- Release: Final Editorial Gate.
- Build voice profile: Voice Profile Builder.

## Hand-off contract
Every hand-off identifies assignment ID, manifest version, workflow level, input artefacts and versions, requested output, editing/retrieval/release authority, stop conditions and human approver. Specialist returns status, output artefacts, unresolved issues, invalidated artefacts and whether human action is required.

## Re-entry rules
- New source or claim change: update claim registry and re-run affected verification.
- Structural rewrite: re-run argument review and gate.
- Material edit after verification: verification becomes stale.
- Edit after gate: gate becomes stale.
- Manifest or approval change: reassess route.

## Failure handling
Unavailable retrieval means unverifiable, not false. Missing thesis stops drafting. Missing approval owner blocks high-risk release. Specialist conflicts are resolved by safety/truth, human meaning, assignment, project, voice, form, then local improvement; unresolved conflicts go to a human.
