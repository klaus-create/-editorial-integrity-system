# Workflow Levels

## Level 0: Direct
Use for low-consequence, short-form writing where intent is clear and factual risk is negligible.

Typical forms:
- informal email
- brief message
- minor wording correction

Required controls:
- preserve meaning
- preserve requested tone
- final surface check

## Level 1: Guided
Use for public-facing or client-facing short-form work.

Typical forms:
- client email
- social post
- marketing caption
- short announcement

Required controls:
- audience and purpose check
- relevant voice or register profile
- anti-slop scan
- final editorial gate

## Level 2: Governed
Use for consequential business, strategic or externally published work.

Typical forms:
- report
- proposal
- business case
- executive presentation
- article
- white paper

Required controls:
- authorship capture
- editorial brief
- argument review
- anti-slop audit
- voice-preserving edit
- factual verification when claims require it
- final editorial gate

## Level 3: Extended
Use for long-form, high-risk or continuity-sensitive work.

Typical forms:
- book
- major manuscript
- investor memorandum
- policy document
- legal-adjacent or regulated publication

Required controls:
- all Level 2 controls
- claim registry
- continuity tracking
- section or chapter briefs
- versioned source packs
- explicit human approval
- audit trail

## Routing rule
Use the lowest level that adequately protects meaning, truth, voice and consequence. Do not impose a heavy workflow merely because the system can.
