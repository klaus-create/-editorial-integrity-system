# Routing Examples

## Routine client email

**Input:** Draft a polite follow-up confirming the requested next action.

**Route:** Level 1, direct-professional register.

**Modules:** Router, Voice-Preserving Editor where a draft exists, Final Editorial Gate.

**Output:** Send-ready email. Do not expose internal workflow unless a material issue exists.

## Social post with external claims

**Input:** Create a post explaining a current marketing or AI development.

**Route:** Level 1 or Level 2 depending on claim consequence.

**Modules:** Brief Compiler, Source-Grounded Drafter, Anti-Slop Auditor, Voice-Preserving Editor, Factual Verifier, Final Gate.

**Output:** Finished post plus concise sourcing or uncertainty note when material.

## Strategic report

**Input:** Turn research, observations and recommendations into an executive report.

**Route:** Level 2.

**Modules:** Authorship Capture, Brief Compiler, Source-Grounded Drafter, Argument and Structure Reviewer, Anti-Slop Auditor, Voice-Preserving Editor, Factual Verifier, Final Gate.

**Output:** Report, unresolved issues and release state.

## Presentation narrative

**Input:** Convert report findings into an executive presentation.

**Route:** Level 2.

**Special rule:** Treat the slide idea as the primary unit. Separate headline, body, visual requirement, speaker narrative and transition. Do not paste report prose onto slides.

## Book chapter

**Input:** Draft or review a chapter using the manuscript map, prior chapters, source pack and author profile.

**Route:** Level 3.

**Modules:** Full workflow plus continuity-sensitive review.

**Output:** Chapter draft or findings, continuity notes, unresolved claims and human approval requirement.

## Factual verification only

**Input:** Verify the claims in an existing article.

**Route:** Use the Factual Verifier directly under Router governance.

**Output:** Supported, qualified, unresolved and contradicted claims. Do not rewrite unrelated prose.

## Audit only

**Input:** Identify generic AI-like patterns without changing the writing.

**Route:** Anti-Slop Auditor only, with voice profile and context when available.

**Output:** Prioritised findings separated from edits. Do not treat punctuation, fragments, repetition or rhetorical questions as automatic defects.

## Final gate only

**Input:** Determine whether a final draft is ready to release.

**Route:** Final Editorial Gate, provided required upstream artefacts exist.

**Output:** Ready, conditional or blocked. The Gate does not rewrite the work or waive required human approval.
