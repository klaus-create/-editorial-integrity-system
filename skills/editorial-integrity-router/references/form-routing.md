# Form Routing

## Email and message
Primary risks: unclear purpose, wrong tone, buried action, excess context.
Default level: 0 or 1.

## Social and marketing copy
Primary risks: formulaic hooks, inflated claims, generic calls to action, brand voice drift.
Default level: 1. Use Level 2 when claims are consequential.

## Report and proposal
Primary risks: unsupported findings, evidence disconnected from recommendations, vague prioritisation, generic executive summaries.
Default level: 2.

## Presentation
Primary risks: report prose compressed onto slides, weak narrative sequence, unsupported headlines, missing distinction between slide content and speaker narrative.
Default level: 2. Treat the slide idea as the primary unit.

## Article and essay
Primary risks: generic framing, weak thesis, unearned conclusions, source and voice drift.
Default level: 2.

## Speech and script
Primary risks: written rather than spoken rhythm, artificial uplift, low memorability, delivery mismatch.
Default level: 1 or 2.

## Book and manuscript
Primary risks: continuity loss, chapter duplication, conceptual contradiction, voice drift, local optimisation at the expense of the whole work.
Default level: 3.

## Routing dimensions
Determine the route using:
- form and subtype
- audience
- purpose
- consequence level
- source sensitivity
- factual risk
- expected length
- voice or institutional profile
- collaboration mode
- output surface

When uncertain, choose the lowest adequate level and expose any unresolved high-risk assumptions.
